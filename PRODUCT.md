# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One private owner uses the Android app to understand today's personal health state
and make practical decisions about training, recovery, sleep, work, and whether a
health-safety signal deserves attention. The primary task is to read a conclusion
quickly, understand the most likely cause, and inspect the personal evidence when
needed.

## Product Purpose

Personal Health Engine turns the owner's wearable history, personal baseline,
reported context, and model-reviewed reasoning into one stable Today judgment. It
uses five fixed product states, explains uncertainty without scores or diagnosis,
supports grounded health Q&A, and preserves append-only history and provenance.
Success means the user can understand what changed, why the product reached its
conclusion, what action is appropriate, and how fresh each supporting fact is.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The voice is natural Simplified Chinese, conclusion-first,
plain-language, and appropriately cautious. It should feel like a private decision
assistant, not a diagnostic device or a motivational fitness product.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a readiness-score dashboard, traffic-light wellness app, competitive
fitness feed, clinical diagnosis portal, or generic AI chat interface. Avoid raw enum
names, statistical jargon, unexplained numbers, decorative alerts, alarmist red states,
and generated copy that changes between refreshes.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the current conclusion and preserve the sealed information order.
2. Use the owner's personal baseline and show exact, dated evidence on demand.
3. Separate health judgment, presentation refresh, and notification thresholds.
4. Prefer stable, specific language over scores, novelty, or filler advice.
5. Make uncertainty and health-safety escalation explicit without diagnosing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All meaning must remain understandable without color alone. Use readable system
typography, sufficient contrast, generous touch targets, semantic controls, bounded
text scaling, and clear loading, empty, error, and retry states. Avoid decorative
motion; any state motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
